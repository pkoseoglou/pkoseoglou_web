About

Experimental nuclear structure

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. My work focuses on the structure of atomic nuclei, with particular interest in the evolution of collectivity and deformation in neutron-rich nuclei.

I use gamma-ray spectroscopy, fast-timing methods, in-beam and decay measurements, and detector systems at international research facilities.

Research

Areas and methods

Nuclear structure

Evolution of deformation and collectivity in neutron-rich nuclei, including isotopes near the N = 104 midshell region.

Lifetime measurements

Fast-timing measurements of short-lived excited states using gamma-ray detector arrays and radioactive or stable beams.

Nuclear spectroscopy

In-beam and decay spectroscopy, Nuclear Resonance Fluorescence, neutron activation analysis, and HPGe detector characterization.

Featured project

Yber

Evolution of deformation in neutron-rich Er and Yb isotopes

Yber combines experimental and theoretical studies to investigate the nuclear structure of erbium and ytterbium isotopes near the N = 104 midshell region and toward the neutron dripline.
